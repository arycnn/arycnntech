About

Bedrock developer on both sides of the game

I'm Aryan Potdar, though most people online know me as Liquid. I'm studying Management and Computer Applications, and I have been building for Minecraft Bedrock since 2022.

I work on both the Marketplace and the server side of Bedrock. Several of my published packs do well in both ratings and sales. As Project Manager at Master Duckies I take Marketplace add-ons from concept to certification, and I run my own network, Crownfall Network, across Asia, North America and Europe.

Before that I spent years on the LifeBoat Network team as Community Manager, Beta Tester, Lead Translator, Moderator and Event Lead. Away from Bedrock I play chess, edit videos and keep picking up new skills. I work a night schedule, so American and European time zones are easy for me.

Owner and Lead Developer

Crownfall Network
Present

  • Bedrock network
  • 3 regions

Founded and built Crownfall Network, a Minecraft Bedrock network running in Asia, North America and Europe with geo-routed DNS and automatic region failover. Game modes include Survival, Bedwars, Skywars, Clan Wars, The Deep and Duels, running on PowerNukkitX, a Folia + Geyser survival server and WaterDog proxies, all sharing one MongoDB database over a private WireGuard mesh.

I wrote the network plugin, engine patches, a custom anti-cheat, the replay and evidence system, ban appeals, clans with cross-region chat, an admin portal, a Discord bot with rank sync, and a player shop with live stats and checkout.
